## 生成提示

Create one square, photorealistic miniature diorama product image based on the supplied garden photograph, NOT a poster. The supplied photo is the sole scene reference. Preserve the broad dark reflective pond at the front and right, the rocky planted peninsula projecting from the left, low sculpted spreading pines on both banks, pale boulders along the water edge, taller birches and conifers behind and a narrow tan path behind the right bank. Compress the distant forest into a selective shallow back row while preserving the front-to-back relationships. Reconstruct physical volume, not a photograph printed onto a slab. Finite natural rounded terrain slice with a very thin believable earth edge; water remains inside the scene with no glass tank. Complete only necessary edge thickness and concealed support; do not invent bridges, buildings, koi, lanterns, or people. No sky plane. Delicate true-to-source pine needles, bark, rock and reflective water material, not plastic or clay. Mild elevated three-quarter view, full model visible, model fills about 70% of width, ample clean pure white studio background, soft grounded contact shadow, studio light consistent across surfaces, most of scene sharp. No text, labels, frames, hands or display case. Output a finished standalone miniature image.

## 视觉复核

已查看输入与输出：池面、左侧岩石半岛、两岸低松、后方高树和右岸路径的主要关系仍可辨认；有薄地形边缘、实体树木、倒影和落地阴影；没有新增桥梁、建筑或玻璃罩。

局限：远景树木和池岸被压缩、补全，不是逐棵逐石复制。模型横向占幅大于提示中的70%，两侧留白偏紧，但主体未截断；海报通过缩小放置改善留白。不将该候选描述为所有风格约束均精确满足。
